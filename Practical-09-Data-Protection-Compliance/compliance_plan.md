# Practical 9 — Data Protection Compliance Plan (India)

## Objective
Develop a readiness plan against India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

## Scope and legal-status caution
This is an educational readiness plan, not legal advice. The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 November 2025 with phased commencement. The commencement notification also phases the Act. As of 22 November 2026, do not assume every substantive obligation is already in force: check the relevant Gazette notification, amendments and commencement date before treating an item as a currently enforceable duty. The 18-month provisions from 13 November 2025 are scheduled for May 2027.

## Core workstreams
1. **Governance:** appoint accountable privacy lead; define escalation and board reporting.
2. **Data mapping:** identify personal data, Data Principals, purposes, systems, recipients, processors and locations.
3. **Notice and consent:** prepare clear, standalone notices; record consent where relied upon; support withdrawal and other applicable lawful uses.
4. **Purpose limitation and minimisation:** collect only data needed for a specified purpose; prevent unrelated reuse.
5. **Security safeguards:** access control, encryption, logging, backups, vulnerability management and staff training.
6. **Retention and deletion:** define retention periods and securely erase data when no longer needed, subject to applicable legal requirements.
7. **Rights handling:** build processes for access to information, correction, erasure, grievance redressal and nomination as applicable under the Act.
8. **Children's data:** identify age-related workflows and applicable parental-consent requirements/exemptions.
9. **Processors and transfers:** maintain processor inventory, contracts and transfer restrictions/conditions applicable at the time.
10. **Breach response:** detect, contain, assess, document and provide notifications in the manner and timelines prescribed by operative law/rules.
11. **Significant Data Fiduciary readiness:** assess whether the organization may be designated and whether additional obligations apply.
12. **Evidence:** maintain policies, records, assessments, training logs and test results.

## 90-day readiness plan

| Period | Action | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–15 | Appoint owners; map systems and data | Governance chart and data inventory |
| Days 16–30 | Review purposes, notices and consent journeys | Gap register and revised notice drafts |
| Days 31–45 | Review access, encryption, logs and vendors | Security and processor assessment |
| Days 46–60 | Define retention, deletion and rights workflows | SOPs and request register |
| Days 61–75 | Create breach playbook and train staff | Incident plan and attendance |
| Days 76–90 | Test controls; resolve priority gaps | Test report and management sign-off |

## Responsibility matrix (example)
- Management: funding, accountability and risk acceptance.
- Privacy lead: inventory, notices, rights and compliance monitoring.
- Security/IT: technical safeguards, logging, backups and incident containment.
- HR/procurement: staff training and processor/vendor governance.
- Product teams: privacy-by-design and data minimisation.
